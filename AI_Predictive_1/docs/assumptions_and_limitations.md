# Engineering Assumptions, Operational Limitations & Safety Disclaimers

---

## 1. Prototype Status & Safety Protection Policy

1. **Non-Certified Advisory System:**  
   This application is an educational, research, and engineering decision-support prototype. It is **NOT** a certified safety instrumented system (SIS), emergency shutdown system (ESD), or certified machine protection system under IEC 61508 / IEC 61511.
2. **Protection System Independence:**  
   Under no circumstances should this software, its machine learning models, or automated agentic workflows be connected directly to emergency shutdown relays, variable frequency drive trips, or physical machinery actuators. All plant safety interlocks must remain completely independent, hardwired, and certified.

---

## 2. Sensor Acquisition & Sampling Limitations

1. **Daily RMS Telemetry vs. Time-Domain Waveforms:**  
   The primary daily dataset provides scalar Root-Mean-Square (RMS) vibration velocity ($\text{mm/s}$) and single daily temperature samples.
   - **What daily RMS CAN do:** Track progressive degradation trends, trigger ISO 10816-7 severity zone transitions, detect seasonal drifts, and identify abnormal global mechanical energy.
   - **What daily RMS CANNOT do:** It cannot reconstruct high-frequency shock impacts, perform envelope demodulation, or prove bearing micro-spalling without raw high-speed time-domain waveform data (typically sampled at $\ge 20\text{ kHz}$).
2. **Frequency Spectra Acquisition Context:**  
   Frequency spectra included in the project are demonstrated using controlled synthetic simulations (3000 RPM / 1500 RPM, 2048 Hz sampling rate, 4-second duration). In actual plants, acquisition requires calibrated accelerometers mounted via stud or magnetic base on rigid bearing housings with anti-aliasing filters.

---

## 3. Data Provenance & Ground Truth Integrity

1. **No Artificial Ground Truth:**  
   In legacy scripts, fault labels were frequently assigned by querying whether vibration exceeded 7.0 mm/s. Using such circular thresholds to train and evaluate ML classifiers creates artificial 100% accuracy claims (data leakage).
2. **Honest Simulation Attribution:**  
   All generated datasets, events, and features are explicitly tagged with `provenance: SYNTHETIC_SIMULATION` along with their random seed and generator version. They are never presented as field-acquired measurements.
3. **Hypothesis vs. Physical Proof:**  
   Diagnostic outcomes generated by the rule engine represent *candidates* (hypotheses) ranked by heuristic evidence consistency. A candidate fault is never labeled as "confirmed" without documented physical teardown or borescope inspection.

---

## 4. Vibration Standards Status & Caveats

1. **ISO 10816-7:2009 Status:**  
   ISO 10816-7:2009 is currently listed as published and under revision. The international project ISO/AWI 20816-7 is actively in development as of October 2026. This system uses the published ISO 10816-7:2009 limits as detailed in Europump 2013 Table 1.
2. **Configuration Completeness:**  
   The system enforces that machines without confirmed category, power rating, and measurement location cannot be assigned a severity zone.

---

## 5. Machine Learning & Explainable AI (XAI) Boundaries

1. **Isolation Forest Behavior:**  
   The unsupervised anomaly detector measures multivariate statistical outlier distance in feature space. An anomaly score indicates that a reading deviates from the machine's baseline cluster; it does not identify mechanical failure mode.
2. **SHAP Interpretation:**  
   SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations) values describe which feature influenced the model's numerical output. A high SHAP value for `P2-A` vibration explains *why the model flagged an anomaly*, but is not physical proof of coupling misalignment.
